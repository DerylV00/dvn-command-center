All running at once.

02 · The Distinction · Never confuse these two
Container
vs. Architect.
The Blaze-Star trap is falling in love with being needed — cooking great meals, fixing things, being the indispensable person in the house — and never stepping into the role that actually changes the trajectory. Know which mode you're in at all times.
The Container — Level 1 tasks
Renovation work and house maintenance
Cooking, cleaning, grocery runs
Personal training sessions
Contractor follow-up calls
Routine email drafting
Small claims court research and filing
Do these well. Stay fully present in them. But ask yourself daily: what am I building inside this container?
The Architect — Level 2 + 3 work
Noticing patterns in Ken's language and behavior
Building and running the Monday session
Translating conversations into wheel dimension data
Maintaining and evolving Atlas
Delegating what belongs to Tempo/Steel people
Designing the October proof-of-concept story
This is the work that scales. This is what only you can do. Protect this time like maker blocks.
03 · The Structure · How your week actually runs
The Perfect
Week.
Your week must be structured around your own wheel first — then Ken's. The architect cannot see someone else's life clearly if his own is running on empty. Your morning is yours before the day is Ken's.
Morning
Every day
Yours first
30–45 min. Non-negotiable. Check-in logged. One wheel dimension reflected — yours, not Ken's. 10 min Spanish minimum. One thing you're being today, not just doing. This is the calibration window.
Monday
Sacred
Morning
Ken session — 2 to 3 hours. You build the agenda. Ken shows up. Review last week's wheel. One dimension for deeper exploration. One behavioral pattern from the week. Intention for the week ahead. This is Level 2 + 3 in full activation.
Rest of day
Level 1 ops. House, training, any contractor follow-up. Translator antenna stays on — log anything that came up in the session within the hour.
Tue–Wed
Operator
Full day
Level 1 in full. Renovations, training, cooking, emails, contractor management, litigation. Do the work with full presence. While working — translator antenna active. Voice note anything notable within the hour. Don't let observations evaporate.
Thursday
Maker + Bar
Morning
Architect block — 2 hrs minimum. Atlas updates. Supabase data review. Monday session prep. Spanish study. This is the protected maker time. No Level 1 tasks in this block unless urgent.
Evening
Bartending shift. Investment quadrant. Act 60 network. After every shift — one voice note: "Who was interesting tonight?" That's it. The network builds one shift at a time.
Fri–Sat
Bar + Recovery
Evenings
Bartending shifts. Condado network. Same rule — one voice note after each shift. Who was interesting? What was the energy? The Act 60 community is being built one conversation at a time.
Daytime
Recovery and Investment quadrant. Spanish. A walk. A read. Something that fills you — not depletes you. The Blaze-Star flame needs fuel. You cannot show up for Ken fully if your tank is empty.
Sunday
Your wheel first
Morning
Review your own wheel before Ken's. Energy, clarity, Spanish — did you hit it? Which of the 8 dimensions did you actually live this week — not just manage? What's one thing that was pure being, not doing? Log it. This is the data that makes you a credible architect.
Then
Build Monday's Ken session agenda. One wheel dimension to explore. One pattern to surface. One intention to set. 20 minutes max prep — then it's ready.
04 · The Practice · Becoming, not just doing
Be and
Do.
You said it directly: "I want to become and be, not just do." This is the Peaceful Warrior principle underneath everything. The warrior does not just act — he is the action. The doing flows from the being. When the being is empty, the doing is hollow.
The Doing — what fills the day
The Tasks.
Renovations. Training. Cooking. Emails. Litigation. Bartending. Atlas builds. Session prep. All necessary. All real. But none of it answers the question of who you're becoming.
Visible. Measurable. Feels productive.
Default mode — the pull is always toward more doing.
Cannot be outsourced entirely — some is genuinely yours.
Trap: mistaking a full day for a meaningful day.
The Being — what builds the architect
The Presence.
Grounded. Clear. Running your own wheel intentionally. Present in the room with Ken — not managing, listening. This is the state where Discernment operates. Gene Key 13 only activates when you're fully here.
Your morning 30 min — before the day belongs to Ken.
The walk. The reflection. The Spanish session.
Full presence in the Monday session — not agenda management.
The moment after a shift: one honest voice note.
"The warrior does not perform actions — he is the action. Drop the doer. Be the movement itself."
