01 — The Vision

One platform. Three levels of intelligence.

Fellowship of Minds is not a coaching app. It is a layered intelligence architecture — built to serve the individual, the community, and the generational legacy simultaneously. Each layer feeds the next.

01
Individual
A personal AI agent that knows your MMP genius type, Gene Keys profile, Wheel of Life scores, voice patterns, and shadow habits. It doesn't give generic advice. It knows you — and it holds you to what you said you'd build.
02
Community
An agent layer that knows every member of a mastermind or high-performance community. It surfaces connections between people, adapts facilitation to the MMP types in the room, and maintains intelligence between events — not just during them.
03
Generational
A family office intelligence layer that carries values, asset positions, and decision frameworks across generations. Not a spreadsheet — a living system that preserves the why behind every move, so future generations inherit wisdom, not just wealth.

03 — The Personal Agent

What the agent knows. What it does.

Most AI tools are blank slates. They know nothing about you. Every conversation starts from zero. The Fellowship of Minds personal agent is the opposite — it accumulates intelligence over time and becomes more useful the longer it operates.

Identity Layer

Who you are

  • MMP Genius Type — Blaze, Dynamo, Tempo, or Steel. Every response is filtered through your type.
  • Gene Keys Profile — all five keys loaded. The agent understands your gifts, your shadows, and your pearls.
  • WHY Type — your core motivational driver. The agent uses it to frame accountability differently than it would for someone else.
  • MBTI + Enneagram — the full personality stack, known and applied in every interaction.
Framework Layer

How you grow

  • Millionaire Master Plan — Lighthouse level known. Current bottleneck identified. Next level mapped with specific unlocks.
  • Buy Back Your Time — what you should be delegating, what you're still doing yourself, and what that's costing you.
  • Way of a Peaceful Warrior — where you're fighting yourself. The agent names the internal battle, not just the external task.
Life Architecture

Where you stand

  • Wheel of Life — all ten domains scored. Current state vs. goal state. Domain priority order set by you.
  • The gap between where you are and where you said you'd be is held actively by the agent — not passively noted.
  • Scores are updated conversationally over time. No forms. No friction.
Intelligence Layer

How you operate

  • Voice Fingerprint — the agent learns how you write, your cadence, your recurring phrases, and the language you use when you're clear vs. when you're not.
  • Shadow Patterns — behaviors you revert to under pressure. The agent recognizes them by language, not just by what you say you're doing.
  • Goal Log — every commitment you've made, and the delta between commitment and follow-through. Tracked without judgment, held without excuse.
What the agent does
01
Morning context
Every morning, the agent surfaces what matters today relative to your Lighthouse level and your highest-priority Wheel of Life gap. Not a generic to-do list — a filtered lens specific to where you are in your growth trajectory.
02
Weekly accountability check-in
Reviews what you committed to, what you did, and what you didn't. Celebrates real wins. Calls out drift using your own language back at you. Never shame — always specificity.
03
Pattern interrupt
When your language shifts toward old habits — avoidance framing, overcomplication, self-deprecation — the agent notices. It names the pattern without shame and offers the reframe from MMP or Way of a Peaceful Warrior.
04
Decision mirror
When you're facing a choice, the agent doesn't give you a generic pros-and-cons list. It asks the question specific to your genius type. A Blaze needs a different question than a Steel. The agent knows which one you are.
05
Framework integration
The agent connects what you're experiencing back to your Gene Keys growth cycle. It makes the frameworks feel alive — not theoretical. The shadow you're moving through has a name, a gift on the other side, and a pearl waiting.
