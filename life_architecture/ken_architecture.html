The growth session
Every two weeks.
One hour. I direct it.

This is not a status update. Biweekly sessions force depth — you only come if you have something real. I run these. I prepare them. I bring what I've noticed in the data, in the house, in the patterns. Ken responds. We calibrate. This is where the mentorship compounds.

The Biweekly Growth Session — Structure
1 Hour · I Direct
What I bring
Health & energy pattern summary from the agent
3 things I noticed in how Ken operates this week
House & ops update — what's moving, what's blocked
One question I want to go deeper on with him
My own growth — what I'm working on, what's hard
What we decide
Priority shifts for the next two weeks
Anything Ken wants to add to the agent / system
Feedback on what's working, what to adjust
One thing Ken wants to explore or experiment with
Where I'm growing into more responsibility

Weekly would turn this into a check-in. Biweekly makes it a session worth preparing for. There's enough time between them to have something real to bring. That's where growth actually compounds — not in frequency, but in depth.
