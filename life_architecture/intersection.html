The role — precisely defined
The connective tissue
is a philosophy.

The person at the center of all three levels doesn't belong to any one of them. They belong to the thread that connects them — Fellowship of Minds. The belief that human connection and AI capability are not opposites. That the most powerful thing technology can do is make genuine presence more accessible, not obsolete. That grounding people in themselves, in each other, and in something larger than the transaction is the work that matters most right now.

That's not a support role. That's the infrastructure role. And it's the one being built.
