05 — The Frameworks
One architecture.
Four lenses.
MMP · Roger Hamilton
Blaze Genius
WHO before HOW
People smart. Summer energy. Winning formula: magnification — growing relationships so the same value reaches more people. Losing formula: calculation — trying to multiply before magnifying. The question before every decision: "How can this only be done with me?" Parallel avenues running. Multiple lanes to lean into.
Gene Keys · Richard Rudd
Gene Key 13
Discord → Discernment → Empathy
I unlock my prosperity through Discernment. I undermine it through Discord. Discernment means hearing beneath what's being said — the hesitation after a confident answer, the topic that keeps almost appearing, the sentence Ken started and redirected. This cannot be automated. It has to be lived into.
Myers-Briggs · ENFJ-A
The Protagonist
83% Extraverted · 79% Assertive
Sees potential in people before they see it in themselves. Leads from confidence, not fear. Shadow: waiting until perfect before shipping — name this when it appears. Shadow: overextending, carrying what isn't yours. Ship before ready. Set the boundary. Name the conflict.
Simon Sinek · Start With Why
WHY → HOW → WHAT
Inside-Out Always
WHY: I believe people are more powerful when connected to something real — land, community, each other, themselves. Everything built exists to deepen those connections. Apply to every major decision: does this start from belief or product? Product without belief is noise.
