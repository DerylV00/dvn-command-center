The pricing reframe · MMP Chapter 5 applied
Not dollars.
Change in quality of life.
Chapter 5 is clear: package, price, and promote value inline with your genius. For Blaze Genius, value is not measured in hours or deliverables. It's measured in the specific, named change in a person's actual life. This reframe governs how FOM Institute talks about every engagement.
Old frame — Extraction
"What can I do for you?"
Service provider language. Positions you as a vendor. The client evaluates hours and deliverables. Invites comparison with cheaper options. Reduces the relationship to a transaction. This is how assistants get hired and replaced.
New frame — Presence
"Who can I be and become for you?"
Life architect language. Positions you as a presence, not a service. The client evaluates change in quality of life, time reclaimed, and progress in each wheel dimension. Invites partnership, not procurement. This is how the relationship compounds.
Old value unit
"$5,000 per month"
A fee for time. The conversation is about whether you're worth the number. Invites negotiation, comparison, and doubt. Positions you in a market of similar services.
New value unit
"Named change in each wheel dimension"
A result. The conversation is about what specifically changed in Health, Relationships, Inner Life, Experiences. Invites reflection and ownership. Positions you in a market of one — because nobody else has the methodology, the agent, and this specific relationship.
"Lean into the edge in each wheel dimension. Confidence level in each house. The value is not time — it is the specific, documented movement from where they were to where they are now."
