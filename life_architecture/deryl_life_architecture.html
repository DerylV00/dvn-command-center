The five-lens extraction — how transcripts become intelligence
Every recorded session gets run through five extraction questions. The output becomes the update to Atlas's knowledge base.
1
Exact quotes for accountability anchors — Not paraphrased. Verbatim. The things he said that reveal what he actually cares about, in his own language.
2
Behavioral patterns — What happened that week that confirms or contradicts a known pattern. Servant leader reflex, reactive scheduling, depth avoidance. Named specifically.
3
Wheel dimension observations — What dimension was he actually in vs. what he said he was in. The gap is always more useful than the stated position.
4
Nudge calibration — Which Atlas nudges landed. Which he never brought up. This is how Atlas gets sharper over time — not through more data, through more accurate data.
5
New commitments and language — Anything he said this session that changes what Atlas needs to hold. A new stated value. A new pattern in what energizes vs. drains him.

Atlas holds the memory. I provide the presence. Discernment cannot be automated.
