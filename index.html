Section 01

Parties

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of June 1, 2026, between Ken Wilkins ("Client") and Deryl VanNostrand ("Contractor").

Section 02

Nature of Relationship

Deryl VanNostrand will serve as an independent contractor to Ken Wilkins. This Agreement does not create an employer-employee relationship. The Contractor is not entitled to employee benefits, workers' compensation, or withholding of taxes by the Client. The Contractor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on compensation received under this Agreement.

Section 03

Scope of Services

The Contractor will provide adaptive life and operations support with one governing priority: to reduce friction in the Client's daily life, protect his time and cognitive bandwidth, and create the operational foundation from which everything else can grow. Services are organized into a phased approach that reflects the natural progression of trust, context, and relationship development.

The guiding principle: no business ideating, mentorship, or strategic collaboration begins until the Phase 1 foundation is proven solid and the working relationship is established. Trust is built through consistency in the small things first.

Phase 1  ·  June – September 2026 Foundation — Free his head, earn his trust
Meal preparation & nutrition
Grocery planning, macro-tracked cooking, and supplement protocol management. The Client's nutrition runs without requiring his attention or decision-making.
Home environment
Cleaning, organization, and upkeep of the Client's residence maintained to a standard that reflects his lifestyle. A clear environment is a clear headspace.
Fitness accountability
Training program design and daily execution alongside the Client. Hip mobility, strength, conditioning, and physical routine anchored in consistency.
Onsite project management
Primary point of contact for all contractors, construction workers, and vendors. The Client is never pulled into a logistics or scheduling conversation that does not require his direct judgment.
Renovation tracking
Progress logged, issues surfaced with proposed solutions, schedules managed. The Client receives a clear summary — not a problem to solve.
Daily operational buffer
Errands, scheduling, vendor calls, and day-to-day logistics absorbed fully by the Contractor so the Client's time and cognitive energy are protected at all times.
Phase 2  ·  October 2026 onward Systems — Personal OS & relationship management
Personal OS build
Connecting the Client's health devices (Oura Ring, Garmin, Whoop) into a unified system. Agent interprets data through the Client's personality profile and delivers one actionable insight per day — not a dashboard, a nudge.
Notion knowledge base
Voice notes, health logs, reflections, and goal progress backlogged automatically and kept searchable. Longitudinal record of development and intention.
Relationship management
Serving as gatekeeper, managing introductions, and ensuring follow-through on the Client's behalf.
Event operations
Director of Event Operations for Growth House Immersion events (primarily April and October).
Intelligence curation
Daily briefing on topics tied to the Client's interests, investments, and the Growth House mission. Relevant, not generic.
Phase 3  ·  By mutual agreement Growth — Business development & strategic collaboration
Business development
Collaborating on identification, research, and launch of new ventures including concierge, hospitality, and tourism — once Phase 1 and Phase 2 systems are proven.
Strategic assistance
Proactively surfacing ideas, proposals, and efficiencies that improve the Client's quality of life and business outcomes.
Mentorship & growth sessions
Once the operational foundation and relationship trust are established, intentional space for personal development, purpose exploration, and leadership evolution.

The Contractor will maintain a proactive task backlog to ensure productive use of time during periods when the Client is traveling or otherwise unavailable. The Contractor is expected to take initiative in identifying and addressing needs without waiting for direction.

Section 05

Term & Amendments

This Agreement shall commence on June 1, 2026 and shall continue indefinitely until terminated by either party or amended by mutual written agreement.

The parties acknowledge that the scope, compensation, and structure of this engagement are expected to grow and evolve as the relationship develops. Either party may propose amendments at any time. Amendments become effective only upon the written consent of both parties. There is no fixed term, no automatic expiration, and no obligation to renegotiate on any schedule unless both parties mutually agree to do so.

Section 06

Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing thirty (30) days written notice. Termination may occur with or without cause.

Upon termination, the Contractor shall be compensated through the final date of services rendered on a pro-rated basis. All confidential information must be returned or destroyed. Sections 7 (Confidentiality) and 8 (Intellectual Property) survive termination indefinitely.

Section 07

Confidentiality

The Contractor agrees to hold all non-public information relating to the Client's personal life, business interests, finances, relationships, ventures, and strategic plans in strict confidence. This includes all information disclosed verbally, in writing, or through observation. The Contractor shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent from the Client, except as required by law.

This confidentiality obligation shall remain in effect indefinitely following the termination of this Agreement. The Contractor acknowledges that the Client's privacy and discretion are of paramount importance in this engagement.

Section 08

Intellectual Property

Any work product, systems, documents, proposals, frameworks, or tools created by the Contractor specifically for the Client or the Client's businesses during the term of this Agreement shall be considered work product of the engagement. Ownership shall be agreed upon in writing on a case-by-case basis, with recognition that the Contractor may develop and retain methods, systems, and frameworks for independent use, provided they do not incorporate the Client's confidential information.

Section 09

Availability & Schedule

  • Primary working days Monday through Friday — full days dedicated to the Client
  • Morning block Cooking, meal prep, and fitness training alongside the Client — foundational daily routine establishing nutrition, physical accountability, and shared rhythm
  • Daytime block Operations, errands, cleaning, logistics, and onsite project management — Contractor serves as primary point of contact for contractors, vendors, and all house-related coordination
  • Embedded systems work AI agent build, health tracking, Notion logging, and Personal OS development integrated into daily operations — built continuously, not scheduled separately
  • Biweekly growth session One structured hour every two weeks — directed by the Contractor. Agenda covers health and energy patterns from the agent, operational updates, observations, and mutual calibration on priorities and direction
  • On-call expectations Not expected to be on-call 24/7 — emergency availability is reserved for genuine urgent matters only
  • Task backlog Contractor maintains a proactive backlog for independent work during Client travel or unavailability — no gaps in productivity
  • Event months During Growth House Immersion months (April & October), full focus shifts to Director of Event Operations duties

The daily structure is designed to compound over time — the morning routine builds physical foundation and trust, the daytime block removes all operational burden from the Client, and the embedded systems work means the Personal OS grows stronger every day without requiring separate dedicated time.

Section 10

Contractor's Independent Business Activities

The Client acknowledges and supports the Contractor's right to develop independent business ventures during the term of this Agreement, including real estate, hospitality, event production, online services, and wellness. The Contractor shall ensure that independent business activities do not materially conflict with or detract from services rendered to the Client.

Where business ventures are developed collaboratively with the Client's resources, network, or involvement, the terms of ownership, revenue sharing, and participation shall be agreed upon separately in writing.

Section 11

Independent Contractor Status

The Contractor retains full independence in determining the method and manner of performing services, subject to reasonable direction from the Client regarding outcomes and priorities. The Contractor is free to perform services for other clients, provided such work does not conflict with obligations under this Agreement.

The Contractor is responsible for providing all tools, devices, and resources necessary to perform services unless otherwise agreed. The Client shall not direct the Contractor's daily schedule beyond agreed check-in points and priority setting.

Section 12

Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico and applicable United States federal law. In the event of a dispute, the parties agree to first attempt resolution through good-faith discussion. If unresolved, disputes shall be submitted to mediation prior to any legal action.

Section 13

Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior discussions, representations, and understandings. This Agreement may be amended only by a written document signed by both parties.
